A workshop has to be ready for one rush order, and does not know which of its two
customers will place it.

Before anything is known, the workshop may reserve overtime slots: up to two for the
morning team at a cost of 4 each, and up to two for the evening team at a cost of 4 each.
Each reserved slot delivers 3 finished pieces for the team it was reserved with.

Then exactly one of the two customers places a rush order — never both, never neither, and
the workshop must be prepared for whichever it turns out to be. The morning team serves
customer 1, the evening team serves customer 2. A rush order raises the affected
customer's requirement by 3 pieces.

Only once the order has arrived may the workshop hire a single freelancer, at a cost of 2,
who delivers 3 pieces for whichever customer needs them. There is only one freelancer
available, so the choice is where to send them.

Customer 1 needs 3 pieces in any case, customer 2 needs 4. Every reservation and the hire
are yes/no decisions. Minimise the total cost in the worst case.
